# One small thing: ring the bell.

Sure, you could press it. Or you could assemble ramps, dominoes, springs, and fans into a gloriously unnecessary chain reaction.

**Overengineered** is a small, grid-based mechanical puzzle game about doing simple things the complicated way.

- Eight puzzles that introduce new parts and increasingly elaborate routes.
- Free build with unlimited parts.
- Tap to place, tap again to rotate. Mouse, touch, and keyboard controls.
- Undo, hints, and a visible trail showing where your machine broke.
- Optional sound and local progress saves.
- No account, ads, or downloads required to play in the browser.

## How it works

Pick a part, place it in a socket, and point its arrow toward the next handoff. Ramps and dominoes move one socket; springs jump two; fans blow three. Visit every brass checkpoint and ring the bell. The rules are deterministic and grid-based, so you can reason about every step.

## Prototype notes

This is version 0.1.0, made by Thomas Verdier. It explores a readable chain-reaction puzzle format rather than realistic rigid-body physics. Feedback on the first three puzzles and the feel of building is especially useful.
