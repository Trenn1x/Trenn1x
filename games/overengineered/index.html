One small thing: ring the bell.

  1. Pick a part. Tap a socket to place it.
  2. Tap a placed part to rotate its arrow.
  3. Run it. Tinker. Revel in the excess.

Arrows show the next landing. Springs skip one socket; fans skip two. Brass rings are checkpoints. Bolted parts stay put.

Keyboard: Tab to a socket, Enter to place or rotate. Space runs the machine. Z undoes.
