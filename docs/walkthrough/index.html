Read the walkthrough

00–05 seconds: A client signs. Give the kickoff a clear next step with one intake, a project brief, assigned tasks, and a welcome draft.

05–13 seconds: Capture the client, service, goal, contact, and proposed kickoff. Harbor & Pine wants more qualified consultation requests from its website.

13–21 seconds: The intake becomes a project brief that carries the goal, client contact, proposed kickoff, deliverable, and context into one shared starting point.

21–29 seconds: Confirm the scope and budget, approval owner, and required access. Approving the brief makes the first task ready.

29–41 seconds: Seven tasks have owners and working-day dates. Completing the scope task unlocks kickoff preparation. Completing that task unlocks page planning and asset collection.

41–49 seconds: Copy a welcome draft with the client’s goal, proposed kickoff, and next steps, ready to personalize and review.

49–55 seconds: Download the project handover in Markdown and the assigned task list as a CSV.
